About

Why we started SafeGuardAI

Today’s teenagers are the first to grow up with algorithmic feeds, and the first who will grow up with AI-generated ones. We believe families deserve tools that are as thoughtful as the systems they are up against.

Our mission

Help young people thrive in a world of intelligent media

SafeGuardAI is developing research-led tools that help families notice harmful patterns early, talk about them openly and seek help when it is needed, without turning parents into surveillance and teenagers into suspects.

Where we are today. Pulse is a concept in development. We are preparing a first pilot and inviting families, schools, clinicians and researchers to help shape it.

SafeGuardAI is a private company based in Singapore. It is not affiliated with any university, hospital or public body.
