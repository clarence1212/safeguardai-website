The pilot

Help shape Pulse from the start

Our first pilot will test whether Pulse can be accurate, useful and trusted by teenagers. It will begin only after independent ethics review, and with teenagers’ assent as well as parents’ consent.

Families

Parents and teenagers aged 13 to 17 who want to help design a tool they would trust.

Schools

Counsellors and leaders interested in early support and digital wellbeing education.

Clinicians

Psychologists, psychiatrists and paediatricians to help define evidence-based thresholds.

Researchers

Academic partners in adolescent development, privacy and AI evaluation.
